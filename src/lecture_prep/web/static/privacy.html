Privacy Policy

Lecture Prep is a personal, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only): the times and titles of the day's events, to find lectures.
  • Google Drive (read-only): the lecture files in the owner's lecture folders.

It never creates, changes or deletes anything in Google Calendar or Google Drive.

What it stores

Course names, lecture numbers and the dates they were taught, the summaries it writes, and a record of which Telegram messages were sent. Lecture files and calendar events are not stored. Questions asked in the chat are not stored.

Who it is shared with

To write summaries and answer questions, lecture files are sent to a language model provider (Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude as a backup). Summaries and reminders are delivered through Telegram to the owner only. Data is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Use of Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
